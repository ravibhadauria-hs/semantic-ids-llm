1. **Click on the Handshake URLs** above to view the actual job postings
2. **Evaluate semantic similarity** - Do the jobs in each group seem semantically related?
3. **Check for consistency** - Are the job types, industries, or requirements similar?

## Expected Results

- **Good clustering**: Jobs with identical three-level semantic IDs should be highly semantically similar
- **Consistent patterns**: Jobs should share common themes like job function, seniority level, or industry
- **Minimal outliers**: Most jobs in a group should be clearly related
- **Hierarchical structure**: The three levels should represent increasingly specific job characteristics
